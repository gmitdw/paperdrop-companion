How PaperDrop keeps things moving

Your receipt is saved on this device first, then sent to your shared OneDrive collection. The Surface reads and files it when it is available.

If you save without internet, reopen PaperDrop when you’re connected. An iPhone can pause a closed app.

To keep PaperDrop on your iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Opening a processed document downloads a copy for offline use.
